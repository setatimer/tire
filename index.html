About Cypress Tire

Your neighborhood tire and auto shop

Cypress Tire says it has served Winter Haven-area customers since 1959 at the same location near Highway 17 and Havendale Boulevard. The shop describes its approach as straightforward: recommend what a vehicle needs, without high-pressure sales tactics.

Its listed specialties range from tire sales and high-speed balancing to automotive repairs and aftermarket upgrades.

  • Family-owned business established in 1959
  • Major tire brands including Michelin, Goodyear, Pirelli, Uniroyal, Toyo, Nitto and BFGoodrich
  • ASE-trained technicians
